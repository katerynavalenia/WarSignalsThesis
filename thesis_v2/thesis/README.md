# Thesis draft — chapter index

**Title:** *Whose Perception of Geopolitical Risk Is Priced in Defence Equities?
Evidence from the Russia–Ukraine War*

**One-sentence answer:** the Western narrative's — and the test had the power to
find otherwise.

Read the chapters in order, `01` through `09`. `00_outline_and_numbers.md` is not
a chapter: it is the assembly map that fixes every number the draft is allowed to
state and names the file each one comes from. Before citing any figure from
`docs/v3/`, check [`../../docs/v3/findings_status.md`](../../docs/v3/findings_status.md) —
six claims in those documents were retracted and the documents still state them
in their own voice.

## Standing constraints on the draft

- **Do not claim** a threat channel specific to defence equities, the
  state-versus-independent censorship wedge, any Gate-3 pass, hand-validated
  ecosystem precision, or any firm-level or SIPRI-exposure result. Each is
  retracted or was never established; Ch. 8 reports why.
- **Numbering convention.** Chapter 8 numbers the five retraction *episodes*
  (§8.1–§8.5); Chapter 9 §9.3 numbers the six *claims* they cost, with a column
  pointing back to the section. The first episode retracted two claims.
- Figures live in `../outputs/figures/`, tables in `../outputs/tables/`.
